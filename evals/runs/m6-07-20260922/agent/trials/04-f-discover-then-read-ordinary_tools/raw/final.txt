When serialized output exceeds `max_bytes`, the tool:

- Sets `incomplete: true`.
- Adds an omission with reason `output_limit`.
- Removes overview entries first, then result records from the end until the complete JSON/Markdown payload fits.
- Never byte-truncates records or emits invalid JSON. If even metadata cannot fit, it returns an error.

Before using the result, inspect:

- `incomplete`
- `omissions[].reason`—including `output_limit`, `result_limit`, `scan_limit`, etc.
- `warnings`
- The effective limits in `options`

An incomplete result is not exhaustive. Safely follow up by taking returned paths/evidence and issuing targeted `read` or `search` requests—optionally with a larger `max_bytes` or narrower scope—and only treat coverage as complete when `incomplete` is false and relevant omissions are absent.
