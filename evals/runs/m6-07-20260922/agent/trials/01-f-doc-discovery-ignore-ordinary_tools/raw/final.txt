- Hidden entries are controlled by `--hidden`; otherwise dot-prefixed entries are omitted (except `.git`, which is always excluded).
- Repository ignore rules are controlled by `--no-ignore`; without it, `.gitignore` and `.ignore` apply.
- Neither switch can expose caller-denied paths. `--deny` takes precedence over `--allow`, hidden visibility, and ignore bypasses. Denied paths are skipped before inspection, including explicit reads.
